## 1. Statutory Definition of "Design"

- **Question:** What does the manual mean by the term 'design'?
- **Grounded Answer:** According to the manual, "design" means only the features of shape, configuration, pattern, ornament or composition of lines or colours applied to any article whether in two dimensional or three dimensional or in both forms, by any industrial process or means, whether manual, mechanical or chemical, separate or combined, which in the finished article appeal to and are judged solely by the eye; but does not include any mode or principle of construction or anything which is in substance a mere mechanical device.
- **Reference:**
  - **Physical PDF Page:** Page 8 (and Page 25)
  - **Section:** Section 02.02.04 Design / Section 04.03.01 Design
- **Supporting Document Snippet:**
  > *"―design‖ means only the features of shape, configuration, pattern, ornament or composition of lines or colours applied to any article whether in two dimensional or three dimensional or in both forms, by any industrial process or means... which in the finished article appeal to and are judged solely by the eye..."* (Section 2(d), Designs Act, 2000)

## 2. Ordinary Application Definition

- **Question:** What is an ordinary application?
- **Grounded Answer:** According to Section 03.03 of the manual, an ordinary application is an application for registration of a design that does not claim priority.
- **Reference:**
  - **Physical PDF Page:** Page 11
  - **Section:** Section 03.03 Type of Applications
- **Supporting Document Snippet:**
  > *"03.03 Type of Applications — a. Ordinary application. An ordinary application does not claim priority."*

## 3. Reciprocity Application and Filing Period

- **Question:** What is a reciprocity application, and what filing period does the manual describe?
- **Grounded Answer:** A reciprocity application claims priority of an application filed previously in a convention country under Section 44 of the Designs Act. The manual describes that such an application must be filed in India within six months from the date of filing in the convention country, and this period of six months is not extendable.
- **Reference:**
  - **Physical PDF Page:** Page 9, Page 11, Page 12, Page 63
  - **Section:** Section 02.02.09 Reciprocity Application & Section 03.03 Type of Applications
- **Supporting Document Snippet:**
  > *"b. Reciprocity application. A reciprocity application claims priority of an application filed previously in a convention country. Such an application shall be filed in India within six months from the date of filing in convention country. This period of six months is not extendable."*

## 5. Four Patent Office Locations in India

- **Question:** Which four Patent Office locations are mentioned in the manual?
- **Grounded Answer:** In India, the Patent Office is located at four locations: **Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, and Chennai**. The manual specifically notes that the Designs Wing is located exclusively at Kolkata, and documents received at the other three offices are forwarded to Kolkata.
- **Reference:**
  - **Physical PDF Page:** Page 9
  - **Section:** Section 02.02.07 Patent Office
- **Supporting Document Snippet:**
  > *"In India, the Patent Office is located at four locations viz. Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata & Chennai. However, Designs Wing is located only at Kolkata. Accordingly, the documents relating to designs received in the other three offices are sent to Kolkata. It may be noted that there is no separate Designs Office in India."*
